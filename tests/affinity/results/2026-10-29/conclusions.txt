REVIEW-CORRECTED CONCLUSIONS: LOCAL CACHE / WORKER / SOCKET EXPERIMENT

This supersedes pre-review/conclusions.txt. Original raw measurements are preserved.
144 successful measurements: 24 cases x six randomized repetitions; 2 s warmup and 10 s measurement.
Client CPUs 8,10,12,14; echo-server CPUs 17,18,20,23; Go 1.25.0, GOMAXPROCS=4.

RETAINED THROUGHPUT OBSERVATIONS
For this synthetic mutable-LRU + encoding + loopback workload, ordinary Go scheduling with owner-local shards outperformed pinned workers in the TCP cases. This is exploratory evidence for this setup only, not a general affinity or gocql/database result.

Balanced TCP median throughput (million 256-byte records/s; 32-record batches):
tcp-64MiB-update1-A-shared: 4.579 M records/s
tcp-64MiB-update1-B-shared-pinned: 1.958 M records/s
tcp-64MiB-update1-C-sharded: 5.566 M records/s
tcp-64MiB-update1-D-sharded-pinned: 4.328 M records/s
tcp-64MiB-update1-E-sharded-pinned-socket: 4.383 M records/s

Within-block paired pinning throughput changes: balanced TCP -22.4%; skewed TCP -21.8%. The CPU-only paired changes ranged from -6.1% to +1.4%, without a dependable pinning benefit. These throughput counts are unaffected by the identified latency sampling bias, but remain specific to the simplified workload.

WITHDRAWN LATENCY CLAIMS
The old every-256-batch sampling repeatedly selected the same input positions. Legacy p99 statistics and all latency ratios are invalid as representative workload latency. The earlier 3.2x p99 claim is withdrawn. Corrected summary.json/report.txt withhold them; pre-review copies are historical only.

SOCKET FEASIBILITY, NOT SUSTAINED AFFINITY
All 48 original socket-option workers already reported incoming CPUs different from their target at measurement start after warmup, and again at completion. The one-time option setting succeeded but did not maintain the requested placement. E is therefore a feasibility probe only. No E/D sustained socket-affinity throughput comparison is supported or emitted by the corrected summarizer.

LIMITS
Every lookup mutates the synthetic LRU. Sharding removes a global mutex and shared LRU; it does not demonstrate hardware affinity benefits or performance of immutable prepared handles. There is no CQL stack, gocql execution, database, TLS or physical NIC in this experiment.
Worker completion variation is not attributed per-vCPU load. The host was shared; hardware cache-miss and full migration counters were unavailable. Do not infer a cache-locality mechanism or universal load-balancing conclusion from these throughput observations.

REVIEW FIXES
The corrected harness uses one random latency sample per 256-batch window plus a full-run reservoir. It records socket status after warmup and during/after traffic, requires unique expected case/repetition identities and paired seeds, and carries repository license/copyright notices.
Original environment.json/source hashes identify the old measured sources; they are not claims that the modified source produced the old data. See review-corrections.json and the separate post-review-validation directory for current verification.
